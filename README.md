# AFTERMODESP Travel & Tourism

A premium responsive travel website built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. White and forest-green color palette, editorial typography, destination showcase and travel experience cards.

## How to view

Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Publish on GitHub Pages

1. Upload `index.html` and the entire `assets` folder to the root of your GitHub repository.
2. In GitHub repository settings, open **Pages**.
3. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
4. Your website URL is shown under Pages when published.

## Before launch

- Destinations are inspiration; travel experiences and prices are **illustrative, not actual offers**.
- The contact button opens a draft in the visitor's default email application, without a recipient. Add your verified business email to the `mailto:` link in `index.html` when available.
- Photos and Google Fonts are loaded from external providers and require internet access. Review licensing and privacy obligations before commercial use.
- Replace sample content with your actual business services, pricing, booking terms, legal details and verified contact channels.
